  • SMS notification to parents after each payment
  • Reports on payments and outstanding balances

Why Real-Time Fee Updates Matter

In many schools, fee payment reconciliation can take days. During that time, parents are uncertain about their balance, and disputes often arise when receipts are delayed.

This system eliminates that gap by generating an SMS notification the moment a payment is recorded, giving parents instant clarity on their new balance.
