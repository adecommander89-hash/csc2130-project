About St. Peter's Budokomi

St. Peter's Budokomi Senior School is a leading educational institution located in Bukhayo West, Bungoma County. We are dedicated to nurturing students to reach their full potential through quality education and strong moral values.

Our Mission

To provide a holistic education that empowers students with the knowledge, skills, and values necessary to excel academically and become responsible citizens. We believe in academic excellence and ensuring that no student is left behind. Our community is built on integrity, respect, and hard work.

Our Vision

To be a center of academic excellence and character development, producing well-rounded individuals ready to tackle global challenges.

Our Core Values

  1. Integrity: Upholding honesty and strong moral principles.
  2. Excellence: Striving for the highest standards in all endeavors.
  3. Respect: Fostering a culture of mutual respect.
  4. Teamwork: Believing in the power of collaboration.

School Glossary

Elimu ni Ngao
The school motto, meaning "Education is a Shield".
Bursar
The school official responsible for managing fees and finances.
HOD
Head of Department, responsible for leading a specific subject area.
Co-curricular
Activities outside the classroom, such as sports and clubs.
