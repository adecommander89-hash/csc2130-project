About St. Peter's Budokomi Senior School

St. Peter's Budokomi Senior School is a premier educational institution located in Bukhayo West, Busia County. We are dedicated to nurturing students to reach their full potential through quality education and strong moral values.

Our History

Founded with the vision of providing accessible and transformative education to the youth of Busia County, St. Peter's Budokomi has grown into a beacon of academic excellence. Over the years, we have consistently produced top-tier students who go on to make meaningful contributions to society.

Our Mission

To provide a holistic education that empowers students with the knowledge, skills, and values necessary to excel academically and become responsible citizens.

Our Vision

To be a center of academic excellence and character development, producing well-rounded individuals ready to tackle global challenges.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity: We uphold honesty and strong moral principles in all our endeavors.
  • Excellence: We strive for the highest standards in academics and co-curricular activities.
  • Respect: We foster a culture of mutual respect among students, staff, and the community.
  • Teamwork: We believe in the power of collaboration and unity.
