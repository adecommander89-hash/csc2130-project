Our Mission

Our mission is to provide St Peter's Budokomi with a fast, accurate, and transparent way to manage student fee payments. We aim to eliminate receipt delays and disputes by instantly notifying parents of their updated fee balance via SMS.

Our Vision

Our vision is a future where every parent knows exactly how much they owe at any moment, and where no teacher wastes hours reconciling payment records. We want fee payment to be as simple as receiving a text message.

Why It Matters

This project uses real-time balance calculation to ensure parents are never left in the dark about their child's fee status. Many schools today struggle with late reconciliations and payment disputes, and this system is built specifically to solve that.

How to use the System

  1. Admin logs into the system with valid credentials.
  2. Register a new student, including their parent's phone number.
  3. Record a fee payment with amount, date, and reference number.
  4. The system automatically calculates the new fee balance.
  5. An SMS notification is generated and sent to the parent.
  6. The admin can view reports and download statements.
